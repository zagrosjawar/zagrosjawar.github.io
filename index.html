AI · Health UX Research Chrome Extension

VITAL

VITAL, short for Verification and Intelligence Tool for Accurate Health Literature, is a Chrome extension prototype for evaluating health and lifestyle claims in real time. It scans visible article content, highlights relevant claims, and presents source support, claim status, and framing analysis in a side panel.

Multimedia Verification Dashboard

VeriDash

VeriDash is a redesigned verification dashboard for analyzing video and image evidence — built for journalists and fact-checkers. It adds OCR text extraction, external geolocation lookup, session saving, and a customizable, card-based layout to speed up multimedia verification.

Figma Plugin Design-Dev Handoff

Change Watcher

Change Watcher is a Figma plug-in concept that tracks design changes after handoff, so developers can see exactly what changed, where, and why. It groups updates by frame, flags edits in real time, and uses an AI assistant to explain each change in plain language.

I'm a UX/UI designer with bachelor's degrees in Information Science and Media and Interaction Design from the University of Bergen. My path into design started with curiosity — working in Norway, I became interested in how digital the country is, from BankID to MinID to everyday online services, and wanted to understand how those systems are actually built.

That curiosity took me into Information Science, where I learned how the web, databases, and information systems actually work. Two courses — Human-Computer Interaction and Interaction Design — pulled me further in, and I decided to go deeper into UX/UI with a second degree in Media and Interaction Design.

Through my studies and self-learning, I've worked the entire design process — human-centered design, the Double Diamond framework, heuristic evaluation, and usability testing — from early research to a working prototype. I design and build: this site is hand-coded, and my case studies — VITAL, VeriDash, and Change Watcher — cover AI, health information, and design-to-development handoff.
